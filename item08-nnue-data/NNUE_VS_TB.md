# Where Stockfish's evaluation disagrees with the tablebases

`nnue_vs_tb.py`, 143 materials, 1000 random legal positions each, 143,000 in all.
Engine: `wsl -d Ubuntu -e /home/scott/mate-engines/bin/sf19`. Seed: `nnue-vs-tb-1`.

Ground truth is Syzygy WDL from the side to move, with a cursed win counted as a draw.
The engine's verdict is win, draw or loss at a centipawn threshold; a mate score is a
win or a loss at any threshold. `static` is the `eval` command, `depth3` is
`go depth 3`. `eval` declines a position whose side to move is in check, so 32,148 of the
143,000 positions carry no static verdict; percentages are over what each arm scored.

**Coverage.** The tables here are 3- to 5-man, so every figure below is 3- to 5-man.
Nothing in this file says anything about 6-man materials.
